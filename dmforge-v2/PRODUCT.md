# DM Forge: product

**What it is.** A single-file web app (with an optional local helper) for a Dungeon Master to run and prepare D&D sessions from the Obsidian vault they already keep. It reads and writes plain markdown notes; nothing is changed until the DM presses Push.

**Who uses it.** One person: an Australian DM on a Mac, in Brave, with Obsidian. Metric units only. They use it about equally at the table (mid-session, glancing, one hand on the mouse) and at the desk (prepping, tidying notes afterwards).

**What they are doing, in order.** Prep a session (read last time, line up scenes, secrets, encounters), run it (log lines, timer, dice, combat, "what did I promise?"), wrap it up (tidy the log, close out, push).

**What good feels like.** Calm. The notes and the one thing being done are in front; everything else is one click away and out of the way. Nothing competes for attention at the table. It should feel like a well-made tabletop book: warm paper, serif titles, a single gold accent.

**What it must never do.** Hide or lose notes, change the vault without a Push, or add anything that makes a mid-session action slower.

**Mode (Impeccable): Operate.** Task completion, scanability and restraint outrank expression.

**Brand and tone.** Warm parchment (light) and brown-black (dark), gold accent per campaign, serif display type with a clean sans for text. Plain, direct copy ("Push changes", "Close out"), no jargon.
